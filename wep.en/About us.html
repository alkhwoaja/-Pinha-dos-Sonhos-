Welcome toPinha dos Sonhos Golf Resort

Enjoy the ultimate golf destination in Portugal, featuring breathtaking views and world-class facilities.

Our story

Nestled in the heart of Portugal's charming countryside, "Pinha dos Sonhos" blends lush green landscapes with breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean. The resort boasts world-class golf courses meticulously designed for all skill levels, alongside luxurious suites offering comfort and privacy. Guests can unwind with serene spa sessions or savor delicious Portuguese cuisine at the resort’s fine dining restaurants. It’s the perfect destination for golf enthusiasts and those seeking tranquility and luxury alike.

The signature courses

The signature golf courses at our resort are masterfully designed to blend natural beauty with sporting challenge. Each course is crafted by world-class experts, featuring diverse terrains that include rolling greens, serene waterways, and breathtaking landscapes. With stunning views of the ocean or surrounding valleys, every swing offers a moment of tranquility and inspiration. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro, these courses offer varying levels of difficulty to suit all skill levels

Services at the Golf Resort

Our golf resort offers a wide range of services designed to ensure an unforgettable experience for our guests. We provide luxury airport transfers, along with professional trainers to enhance your golfing skills. The resort also features a luxurious spa offering massage and relaxation sessions, as well as restaurants serving delicious local and international cuisine. For ultimate comfort, our 24/7 concierge service is available to cater to all your needs.

Digital Courses

At our resort, we offer innovative digital courses designed to help guests enhance their golfing skills regardless of their level. Whether you're a beginner or a professional, you can benefit from interactive training programs that include high-quality video lessons, performance analysis using virtual reality technology, and advanced digital tools to track your progress. These courses provide a flexible learning experience tailored to your schedule, allowing you to learn and enjoy the game anytime, anywhere.

Restaurants

Our resort offers an exceptional dining experience through our luxurious restaurants that cater to all tastes. Whether you're looking for traditional Portuguese dishes or international cuisine, our restaurants feature a diverse menu reflecting the highest quality and flavor. Each meal is prepared using fresh local ingredients, with stunning views of the golf courses or surrounding landscapes. We also provide healthy and customized dining options to meet the unique needs of our guests.
